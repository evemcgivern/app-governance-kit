Application governance, made traceable

Application governance: portfolio, AI, and access controls, traceable to ISO/IEC 19770, COBIT 2019, ISO/IEC 27001, and ISO/IEC 42001.

Three areas, one set of tools

Application portfolio management

I keep the application inventory accurate, assign ownership, and run rationalization reviews so a portfolio doesn't quietly outgrow what anyone can govern.

AI governance

I intake new AI systems, score their risk, and keep them inventoried and reviewed alongside the rest of the software estate, not tracked separately or missed.

Access controls

I run periodic access reviews, catch orphaned and excessive access, and hold privileged accounts to a shorter, tighter review cycle.
