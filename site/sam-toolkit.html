SAM toolkit

Forty-one templates and checklists for running a software asset management practice by hand. They open in Excel, Google Sheets, or any text editor. Most files trace to a clause in ISO/IEC 19770-1:2017; the purchasing, savings, and practice files trace to crosswalk rows. All example data is fictional.

All forty-one files, nine examples, and two workbooks (ZIP) Start with the playbook Read the guide Thirteen setup prompts

Where to start

Read the playbook first. It is the one order to follow: ten steps, each with the files to use, the matching setup prompt, and what done looks like. Track the work in the application management workbook, and look up unfamiliar terms in the glossary. The other pages on this site are tools the playbook points to, not a second order.

The guide is the reference for every file and column. These are templates, not a platform: they do not find installs or read license terms, and a person still makes each decision.

Application management workbook

One workbook that takes your program from the As-Is assessment to measurement: assess, plan the gaps, record measures with evidence, read a scorecard, and re-score over time. Formulas only, no macros, and it works in Excel, Google Sheets, and LibreOffice. Crosswalk XW-003 and XW-025.

About the source

The files follow the structure of ISO/IEC 19770-1:2017 (IT asset management systems: requirements). The wording is paraphrased and does not replace the standard, which you can buy from ISO or your national standards body. No affiliation with or endorsement by ISO or IEC. The method behind these files is on the crosswalk explorer; the standards are explained in the field guide.
