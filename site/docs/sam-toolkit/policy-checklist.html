Policy checklist

A checklist for testing a draft ITAM policy before it goes for approval, and again at each review. Complete one copy per policy and keep it with the policy's change history. A policy that fails an item goes back to its owner, or a named person accepts the gap in writing.

Clause references are to ISO/IEC 19770-1:2017 (paraphrased) and to the shared crosswalk (XW- rows). This checklist does not give legal advice; where an item says counsel, have counsel review it. For the fill-in structure see Policy template, and for a draft to practice on see Policy draft.

Policy: ______________________ Owner: ______________________ Date: ___________

Part 1: Ownership and scope

Part 1: Ownership and scope
#QuestionResultEvidence
P1Are an owner and an approver named by role? XW-004 (5.2)
P2Is the sponsor's approval dated and filed? XW-027
P3Is the scope bounded, with exclusions and reasons, and without phrases such as where appropriate? XW-003
P4Is every term a reader could dispute defined?

Part 2: What the policy says

Part 2: What the policy says
#QuestionResultEvidence
P5Does it commit to meeting applicable requirements and to improving? (5.2)
P6Does it cover license compliance and acceptable use? XW-007
P7Can every rule be met by the people it binds, and tested by a reviewer?
P8Are roles mapped, with one accountable role per activity? XW-004 (5.3)
P9Does the exception path name an approver, an end date, and a log entry in Authorization log template? XW-009 XW-019
P10Are penalties stated, with the wording marked for counsel's review? Run this checklist before counsel reads the draft, and record counsel's sign-off date before approval. Decision needed: counsel.
P11Is it consistent with other policies, with conflicts recorded and an owner for each? XW-003

Part 3: Publishing and awareness

Part 3: Publishing and awareness
#QuestionResultEvidence
P12Is it published in a controlled, versioned location, with older versions withdrawn? XW-020 XW-030 (7.6.5)
P13Does a communication plan say what, when, to whom, and how? XW-030 (7.4)
P14Does awareness go beyond onboarding, with acknowledgments tracked? XW-030 (7.3)

Part 4: Review and approval

Part 4: Review and approval
#QuestionResultEvidence
P15Are the review date and the events that bring it forward named? XW-025
P16Are approval and change history kept? XW-020 (7.6.5)
