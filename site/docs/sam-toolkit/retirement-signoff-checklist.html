Retirement sign-off checklist

Download Word (.docx) Download Excel (.xlsx)

What you tick or type stays in this browser. The Word and Excel downloads are blank copies. To keep your answers, use Download my answers (Word), or print this page or save it as PDF.

A checklist to complete before a software application or a device is closed out as retired. Complete one copy per asset, or per batch of identical assets, and keep it with the asset record. Retirement is not done until every function below has signed, or a named person has accepted a recorded exception.

Clause references are to ISO/IEC 19770-1:2017 (paraphrased) and to the shared crosswalk (XW- rows). This checklist does not interpret contracts or give legal advice; ask counsel where a term is unclear.

Part 1: Is it ready to retire?

Part 1: Is it ready to retire?
#QuestionResultEvidence
R1Has the owner confirmed in writing that the business need has ended, or has been replaced? XW-010
R2Is there a retirement decision on record, with the decider and date? Use Authorization log template. XW-010 XW-020
R3If a contract covers it, has the notice deadline been checked, so you are not paying for it after it is retired? Check Renewal calendar template. XW-008 XW-013

Part 2: Sign-offs

Each function confirms its own part. One person can hold more than one role in a small organization, but the question still needs its own answer.

Part 2: Sign-offs
#FunctionWhat they confirmSigned byDate
R4ITAccess is removed, the asset is out of service, and dependent systems are not affected. XW-010
R5FinanceThe asset is off the books, any write-down is recorded, and the final cost is known. XW-012
R6HRNo current employee still depends on it, and any leaver records are updated where the asset held them.
R7ProcurementContract end, notice, and any return, buy-back, or termination terms are handled. XW-013
R8LegalRetention duties, holds, and contract exit terms are met, and no open audit or dispute depends on the asset. Decision needed: counsel, where in doubt. XW-020

Part 3: Evidence

Part 3: Evidence
#QuestionResultEvidence
R9For software: were the licenses it used released back to the pool, or recorded as ended, in License position template? Check the contract's reassignment rights first, because some licenses cannot move to another user or device. Have the contract owner confirm. XW-007
R10For hardware: were any software licenses tied to the device reclaimed before it left? Check the contract's reassignment rights first, because device-tied licenses may not move. Have the contract owner confirm. XW-007 XW-010
R11Was data wiped or destroyed by a method suited to the asset, with a certificate kept? Encryption alone is not wiping. XW-010
R12Was the data it held archived, migrated, or deleted as agreed with the data owner, with a retention period? XW-028
R13Is the asset record updated to retired, with retired_on filled in? Use Asset record template. (8.3) XW-001
R14Is the final cost recorded, so the savings or ROI can be reported later? Use Savings report template. XW-012
R15Are the wipe certificate and sign-offs filed where an auditor can find them, for as long as the records schedule requires? XW-020
