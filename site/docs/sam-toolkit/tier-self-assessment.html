Your assumed tier: Answer every question to see your tier.

A short check of how far your IT asset management has got, using the three tiers in ISO/IEC 19770-1:2017 Annex B. The tiers are cumulative: tier 2 expects tier 1, and tier 3 expects both. Use this as a starting point before the full ITAM maturity check.

How to use it: answer each question yes, partly, or no, and write down the evidence you could show an auditor. A tier counts as met when no question is a "no" and at most one is "partly". Your assumed tier is the highest tier you meet, along with every tier below it. The first tier you do not meet is your next piece of work.

The Annex B tier names and aims are summarized here. Which Annex A processes sit in which tier is shown in Figure B.2 of the standard. Check that figure in your copy before using this to scope a certification.

Aim: know what you have so you can manage it, with reasonable assurance of license compliance.

Gap report

List every question you answered "no" or "partly", with the step that closes it. The web page builds this from your answers, and the Excel workbook has a Gap report sheet you can filter. In Word, write your answer in the third column and delete the rows you do not need.

Gap report
#QuestionYour answerWhat closes the gapOwnerDue date
1.1A record for every asset, with an ownerBuild one record per asset in scope and name an owner for each. Start from the asset record template.
1.2Each record says whether the asset is authorizedAdd an authorized yes/no field to every record and fill it from purchase and approval evidence.
1.3Records verified against what is installedSet a schedule (at least quarterly) to compare records with discovery data, and log the differences found.
1.4Entitlement records heldCollect licenses, contracts, and proofs of purchase into one entitlement list per product.
1.5Entitlements compared with usageRun the license position workbook on a schedule and give each shortfall or surplus an owner.
1.6A measurable target for data accuracyPick a measurable accuracy target, such as the share of records verified this quarter, and report against it.
1.7Changes to the asset record controlledRequire an authorization entry before any record changes, and log who changed what and when.
1.8Security applied to the asset dataLimit who can view and edit the asset data, and review that access on a schedule.
2.1Specification and approval before purchaseAdd a request step that records the need and an approval before any purchase.
2.2Acquisitions recorded when they happenMake creating the asset record a step of buying, not a later cleanup.
2.3Release and deployment update the recordAdd a record-update step to the release and deployment checklists.
2.4Use monitored, including cost-effectivenessTrack usage for each major asset and review its cost against its value on a schedule.
2.5Retirement is a defined stepWrite a retirement procedure that covers disposal, data removal, and closing the record.
2.6Authorizations and their execution loggedUse the authorization log template for approvals and for who carried them out.
3.1Suppliers and contracts managedKeep a vendor and contract register, and check compliance with contract terms as well as licensing.
3.2Costs and values tied to budgetsRecord the cost and value of each asset and tie them to budget lines.
3.3Service levels definedList the services ITAM depends on and record the agreed service level for each.
3.4Other risks managedRun an ITAM risk assessment, record the treatment for each risk, and check that it worked.
3.5Performance measured and reportedDefine financial and non-financial ITAM measures and report them to management on a schedule.
