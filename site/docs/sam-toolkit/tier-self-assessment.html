Tier self-assessment

Download Word (.docx) Download Excel (.xlsx)

Your assumed tier: Answer every question to see your tier.

A short check of how far your IT asset management has got, using the three tiers in ISO/IEC 19770-1:2017 Annex B. The tiers are cumulative: tier 2 expects tier 1, and tier 3 expects both. Use this as a starting point before the full ITAM maturity check.

How to use it: answer each question yes, partly, or no, and write down the evidence you could show an auditor. A tier counts as met when no question is a "no" and at most one is "partly". Your assumed tier is the highest tier you meet, along with every tier below it. The first tier you do not meet is your next piece of work.

The Annex B tier names and aims are summarized here. Which Annex A processes sit in which tier is shown in Figure B.2 of the standard. Check that figure in your copy before using this to scope a certification.

Tier 1: Trustworthy data ("know what you have")

Aim: know what you have so you can manage it, with reasonable assurance of license compliance.

Tier 1: Trustworthy data ("know what you have")
#QuestionYes / partly / noEvidence
1.1Is there a record for every software and hardware asset in scope, with an owner? (8.3, 7.6.2)
1.2Does each record say whether the asset is authorized? (8.3)
1.3Are records verified against what is actually installed or in use, on a schedule? (8.3)
1.4Do you hold entitlement records for the licenses you own? (8.4)
1.5Do you compare entitlements with usage on a schedule, and act on the gaps? (8.4)
1.6Is there a measurable target for data accuracy? (6.2.3)
1.7Are changes to the asset record controlled? (8.2)
1.8Are security and access controls applied to the asset data itself? (8.5)

Tier 2: Life cycle integration ("greater efficiency across the life cycle")

Aim: greater efficiency and cost-effectiveness across the asset life cycle.

Tier 2: Life cycle integration ("greater efficiency across the life cycle")
#QuestionYes / partly / noEvidence
2.1Do requests go through a specification and approval step before purchase?
2.2Is every acquisition recorded when it happens, not later?
2.3Do release and deployment steps update the asset record?
2.4Is use of assets monitored, including for cost-effectiveness?
2.5Is retirement a defined step with disposal and record keeping?
2.6Are authorizations and their execution logged (who, when, why)? (7.6.3)

Tier 3: Optimization ("focus on cross-cutting processes")

Aim: greater efficiency and effectiveness through the cross-cutting functional processes.

Tier 3: Optimization ("focus on cross-cutting processes")
#QuestionYes / partly / noEvidence
3.1Are supplier relationships and contracts managed, including compliance with terms beyond licensing? (8.7)
3.2Are asset costs and values tracked and tied to budgets?
3.3Are the service levels that ITAM depends on defined and recorded?
3.4Are risks not covered elsewhere managed, with the treatment checked? (6.1.2, 6.1.3)
3.5Is ITAM performance measured and reported, financial and non-financial? (9.1)
