# Halden Software Governance Policy: draft

## Purpose
Halden Logistics uses software to run its warehouses, its fleet, its customer service, and its back office. This policy sets the rules for choosing, buying, using, and retiring that software. Good rules help the company spend its money well, keep its data safe, and avoid surprises when a vendor asks for proof of what Halden is entitled to use. The company wants a simple, practical approach that supports the people who do the work and does not slow them down.

## Scope
This policy applies to software used at Halden where appropriate.

## Principles
- Software that supports critical operations is a managed asset.
- Staff should use approved software whenever possible.
- Significant purchases need a business reason.
- Assets are tracked from request to retirement.
- Decisions about software should be fair, open, and based on evidence.
- Good governance is a shared commitment across the whole company.

## Rules
1. Every application must be recorded in the inventory before anyone uses it, and the record must show what the tool is for and which team relies on it.
2. Every request for new software goes through the software request form. The request must show that the tool is secure and fit for the job, and that it does not duplicate a tool Halden already has.
3. Renewals are reviewed before the contract date, and unused software is retired promptly once nobody needs it.
4. All software must be registered, patched, and licensed, with a complete and accurate inventory kept at every moment, including the minute a new tool is installed.
5. Retired software has its data and access removed, and the retirement is recorded in the inventory.
6. Staff must keep to this policy and ask questions if they are unsure about any rule or any request.
7. Vendors who supply software to Halden are expected to follow the spirit of these rules in their dealings with the company.

## Responsibilities
Everyone at Halden shares responsibility for good software governance. Managers support their teams in following the rules, and the technology group helps where it can. The governance council described in its own charter looks at the more difficult requests. Staff who see a problem with a tool or a contract are encouraged to say so early, so that it can be dealt with before it grows.

## Communication
This policy is available to staff on the company intranet. Questions can be sent to the technology group at any time.

## Approval
Approved by company leadership.
