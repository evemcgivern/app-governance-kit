# Code of conduct

We want this to be a place where people can ask questions and fix mistakes without being made to feel small.

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks, in any form.
- Do not share other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, and pull requests that break these rules, and may block
people who keep breaking them. To report a problem, email evemcgivern@gmail.com.
