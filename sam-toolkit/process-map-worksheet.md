# Is and Should process-map worksheet

A worksheet for mapping how one governance process works today (the "Is" map) and how you want it to work (the "Should" map), so the gap between them becomes your list of work. Use one copy per process: for example application intake, renewals, audit response, or retirement.

Do this before you buy a tool or write a policy. A tool or policy built on a process nobody has described writes down guesses. Clause references are to the shared crosswalk (`XW-` rows).

## Step 3: List the gaps

Every difference between the two maps is a gap. Name its type: a missing step, no owner, no record, the wrong order, or manual work that could be reliable. Rank the gaps by the risk or cost of leaving them.

## Step 4: Check the scope

Gaps tend to grow once people see them. Before you commit, check that each fix belongs to this process and has an owner. Anything that expands the scope goes through a change log: the request, who asked, the effect on effort, and who decided. [[XW-025]]
