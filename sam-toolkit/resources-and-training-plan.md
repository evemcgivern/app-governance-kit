# Resources and training plan

A plan for what the ITAM program needs in people, data, training, and tools, and for showing that the people doing the work are competent. Complete one copy a year, when you set the budget, and review it when scope changes.

Clause references are to ISO/IEC 19770-1:2017 (paraphrased) and to the shared crosswalk (`XW-` rows). The standard's requirements on resources, competence, and awareness sit in clauses 7.1, 7.2, and 7.3. This plan sets no price and no head count; use your own figures, and write Not found where you don't have one.

## What to budget

Four things cost money or time. Budget each one, even if the figure is zero.

- **People.** The hours of the program owner, of the data owners who correct their records, and of procurement, finance, and legal staff who take part in routines. A part-time owner still needs protected hours. [[XW-004]]
- **Data sources.** Exports and access to the sources in `data-sources-guide.md`: discovery or inventory output, sign-in logs, accounts-payable data, contract records, and HR lists. Note any cost for access, storage, or staff time to prepare them.
- **Training.** Courses, exam fees, staff time away from work, and the time to run awareness sessions for the people your policies bind.
- **Tools.** Spreadsheets, a repository for records, and any tool you buy or already own. Name what you already pay for before you ask for more.

## Who needs what training

Match each group to what they need to do their part, and name who gives it. [[XW-004]]

| Group | They need to know | Suggested form | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Program owner | The standard's main ideas, license metrics, how to read a contract term, how to build a position | A recognized course or certification, plus practice with your own records | A completed course, and a position reviewed by a second person |
| Data owners | What a correct record looks like, and when to update it | A short walkthrough | A sample of their records that passes `rolling-internal-check.md` |

## What shows competence

Keep two kinds of evidence of competence: a certificate, where the person holds one, and a record of work done in your organization. [[XW-025]]

- A course or exam record, with the date and the provider's name.
- A piece of real work that someone else reviewed, such as a license position or a retirement sign-off.
- A passed sample in `rolling-internal-check.md` for the records the person owns.
- A backup who has done the task once with the primary watching, noted in `audit-response-roles.csv`.

If there's no evidence for a person who needs it, plan the training, set a date, and record the gap in `documentation-gaps-log.csv` until it closes.

## Keep the plan alive

- Review it each year with the budget, and when a new policy, a merger, or a move to cloud changes the work.
- Track acknowledgments and training dates in one place, and check them at each quarterly review. [[XW-030]]
- An acknowledgment shows people read the policy. Also check that they understood it, as the policy lifecycle SOP describes, and record the result.
- Record in the plan any person who leaves a role that holds knowledge no one else has.
